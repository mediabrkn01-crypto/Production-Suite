1. Who we are

Broken English (brokenenglish.in) is an English-language training organisation. Broken English Live Classes is part of the Broken English ERP, our internal academic and administrative platform at work.brokenenglish.in. Broken English is responsible for the information described here.

The ERP is used only by authorised Broken English employees (the Academic team, trainers, HR, sales and management). Students do not sign in to it.

2. Information we collect

Employees (ERP users)

  • Name, work email address, employee ID, role and department
  • Employment records needed to run the organisation, such as attendance, leave and payroll
  • Sign-in and activity records (for example who created, changed or cancelled a class, and when)

Students

  • Name, email address and phone number, the batch and course they are enrolled in
  • Class schedules, class attendance and progress records

Classes

  • Class date, time, duration, batch, assigned trainer and status (scheduled, postponed, cancelled, completed)
  • The Google Calendar event ID, Google Meet link and meeting code created for each class

From Google, when a Google account is connected

See Google user data below for the exact list.

3. How we use information

  • Authenticate authorised employees and control what each role can see and do
  • Schedule classes and keep schedules up to date
  • Create Google Calendar events and Google Meet links for classes
  • Invite the trainer and students to their classes
  • Manage Academic live classes (postpone, reschedule, cancel, reassign)
  • Suggest and record class attendance and class duration
  • Run HR, payroll and other internal administration for employees
  • Keep the system secure and keep an audit trail of changes

We do not sell personal information and we do not use it for advertising.

4. Google user data

Broken English Live Classes lets a trainer — or the Academic team's central class account, classesbrk@gmail.com — connect a Google account once, through Google's own sign-in and consent screen, so that class meetings are created in that account. We never ask for, receive or store a Google password.

What Google data we access

Permission (scope)Data accessedWhy
openid, https://www.googleapis.com/auth/userinfo.emailThe connected account's email address and Google account IDTo record which Google account is connected and show it to the trainer (for example “Join using trainer@gmail.com”)
https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.eventsThe Calendar events this app creates for classes: title, time, description, guest list and Meet linkTo create, update and cancel class events and invite the trainer and students
https://www.googleapis.com/auth/meetings.space.createdThe Google Meet spaces this app creates for classes and their settingsTo create each class's Meet space so the class trainer is the host and can admit students
https://www.googleapis.com/auth/meetings.space.readonlyFor those class Meet spaces only: the meeting report — participant display names and the times each participant joined and leftTo suggest attendance and actual class duration after a class

We do not access other events in your calendar, Gmail, contacts, Drive files, or Meet recordings, transcripts or chat.

How we use it

  • Calendar events and Meet links are created only for classes scheduled by the Academic team in the ERP.
  • Attendance information from Meet is a suggestion: the trainer or Academic team reviews and confirms it before it becomes the class attendance record.
  • Google data is used only to provide these class features. It is not used for advertising, not sold, and not used to train any AI model.

What we store

  • OAuth refresh token for each connected account — encrypted (AES-256-GCM) on our server. The encryption key is held only on the server; tokens are never sent to a browser.
  • The connected Google email address, Google account ID, granted permissions and connection date.
  • For each class: the Calendar event ID, Meet link, meeting code, which account organised it, and sync status.
  • After a class: participant display names, join/leave times and minutes attended, used for the attendance suggestion and the confirmed attendance record.

Short-lived access tokens are kept only in server memory and are never stored.

How long we keep it

  • Refresh tokens: until the account is disconnected (in the ERP or from Google), the employee leaves Broken English, or Google revokes access — then deleted or marked revoked so they can no longer be used.
  • Class event IDs, Meet links and attendance records: as long as needed for the student's course and our academic and administrative records (see Retention).

How to revoke access

  • In the ERP: Academic → Live Classes → Google Meet account → Disconnect. This revokes the token at Google and removes it from our server.
  • From Google at any time: myaccount.google.com/permissions → Broken English Live Classes → Remove access.
  • By email to classesbrk@gmail.com.

After access is removed, new classes are not created in that account. Calendar events already sent stay in guests' calendars as normal Google Calendar events.

5. Limited Use disclosure

Broken English Live Classes' use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

In particular, Google user data is used only to provide the class scheduling, Meet and attendance features described above; it is not transferred to others except as needed to provide those features, for security, or to comply with law; it is not used for advertising; and no person reads it except authorised Academic staff handling the classes concerned, for security investigations, or where required by law.

6. Sharing

  • Google — class details (title, time, guest emails) are sent to Google Calendar and Google Meet to create the class meeting. Google sends the calendar invitations.
  • Authorised Broken English staff — only according to their role (for example a trainer sees their own classes; the Academic team sees all classes; HR sees employee records).
  • Service providers that run the application — Supabase (database, server functions and storage), GitHub Pages (hosting of this website) and our email delivery provider (Brevo) for system emails. They process data only on our behalf to operate the service.
  • Legal — where required by law or to protect the rights and safety of our students and staff.

We do not sell or rent personal information.

7. Storage and security

  • Every ERP page requires an employee sign-in; access is checked against the employee's role and status, and server functions re-check it on every request.
  • Google OAuth tokens are encrypted at rest with AES-256-GCM and kept in a server-only table that browsers cannot read. Google client secrets are stored only as server secrets.
  • All traffic uses HTTPS. The database uses row-level security.
  • Changes to classes are recorded in an audit log.

No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect employee, student and class information.

8. Retention

We keep information only as long as needed for legitimate academic and administrative purposes: student and class records for the duration of the course and the period afterwards required for certificates, queries and our records; employee records as required for employment and legal obligations. Google tokens are kept only while the account is connected. When information is no longer needed it is deleted or anonymised.

9. Your choices, revocation and deletion

  • Disconnect a Google account at any time (see How to revoke access).
  • Ask to see, correct or delete your personal information, or to disconnect your Google account, by emailing classesbrk@gmail.com. We respond within 30 days. Some records may need to be kept where the law requires.

10. Students and minors

Students do not use or sign in to Broken English Live Classes. Their information is entered by Broken English staff to run their enrolled classes. Where a student is under 18, enrolment is arranged with a parent or guardian, who may contact us for the requests above.

11. Changes

We may update this policy when the service changes. The “Last updated” date above shows the latest version. Significant changes affecting Google user data will be made before any new use begins.
