What Broken English Live Classes does

Broken English Live Classes helps our academic team and trainers:

  • Schedule online classes for each batch
  • Create a Google Calendar event for every class
  • Generate the Google Meet link for each class
  • Invite the assigned trainer and the batch's students
  • Update or cancel classes when the schedule changes
  • Share joining information with trainers and students
  • Review class attendance and meeting duration after each class
  • Manage Academic operations from the Broken English ERP

About Broken English Live Classes

Broken English Live Classes is part of the Broken English internal academic management platform (the Broken English ERP). It helps authorised academic staff and trainers manage scheduled online classes and connect those classes with Google Calendar and Google Meet.

Who uses it: Broken English employees only — the Academic team (Academic Head, class coordinators, operations) and our trainers. Students do not sign in to this application; they receive normal Google Calendar invitations and join classes with the Meet link.

Only authorised Broken English staff can access the internal ERP. Every ERP screen requires an employee sign-in. No employee, student, attendance or class data is shown on this public page.

How we use Google services

Broken English Live Classes integrates with Google services to run online classes. A trainer (or the Academic team's central class account) connects a Google account once through Google's own sign-in and consent screen. We never ask for, see or store a Google password.

With the permissions granted, the system uses Google only to:

  • Identify which Google account was connected (its email address)
  • Create, update and cancel the Google Calendar events for scheduled classes — only the class events this app creates
  • Generate a Google Meet link for each class, owned by the class trainer so the trainer can admit students
  • Add the trainer, the students and the Broken English review account as invited guests, and let Google Calendar send them the invitation
  • After a class ends, read that class's Meet attendance report (who joined and for how long) to suggest attendance and class duration, which the trainer or Academic team then confirms

We do not read other events in your calendar, your email, contacts, files, or meeting recordings and transcripts. Google data is never sold or used for advertising.
